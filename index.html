      How to play

      • 🕹️
        Move and jump. Phone: drag the left side to move, tap JUMP, drag the right side to look. Computer: W A S D or arrows, Space, drag the mouse or Q / E.
      • 🚩
        Green flags are checkpoints. If you fall or get hit, you start again from the last one.
      • 🟥
        Red bricks, lasers and fireballs send you back to the checkpoint.
      • 🌀
        Spinners knock you away. Red glowing spinners send you back.
      • 🔨
        Hammers and pistons shove you off. Watch their rhythm first.
      • 🧹
        Sweepers (Hard and Extreme) are striped bars that spin low over a block. Jump over them.
      • 🥁
        Bumpers are squishy pink drums that slide around and bounce you away.
      • 🧱
        Wall Rush: walls with gaps rush toward you. Run to the gap or jump the low yellow blocks, or you get shoved off the end!
      • 🟥
        Falling Floor is a survival game too: every tile you step on drops a moment later. Keep moving and plan your path!
      • 🌀
        Spin Arena is a survival game: stay on the round floor while bars sweep and bumpers slide. The outside ring falls first, so move to the middle! The last 5 standing win.
      • 🐬
        DIVE throws you forward flat. Use it in the air to reach a far edge or save a short jump. Phone: yellow DIVE button. Computer: Shift or F.
      • ⭕
        A red circle on the floor means a block is about to drop there. Wait, then run.
      • 🟧
        Orange blocks crumble a moment after you land on them.
      • 🟪
        Purple blocks blink on and off. They flash white just before they vanish.
      • 🌸
        Light pink blocks shrink and grow. Land while they are big.
      • 🪟
        Glass bridges hide one fake tile in each row. When you reach one, the safe tiles flash green for a moment. Remember them! Land on the platform before it again to see them again.
      • 🌬️
        Fans blow you sideways. Fans with purple on both sides switch direction.
      • 👻
        Ghost blocks (Extreme) are almost invisible until you get close.
      • 💥
        Cannon launch (Extreme finale). Walk into a cannon, watch the dotted line, and tap JUMP or press Space to fire when it points at the next island. One island slides side to side.
      • 😈
        Sneaky blocks (Extreme) look normal but drop away after you land. Keep moving.
      • ⚡
        White striped pads give you a speed boost. Hot pink pads bounce you up high.
