Sky Hop Legends Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Sky Hop Legends is a game for players of all ages. We built it to collect as little information as possible.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it has no accounts or sign-in.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (coins, characters, items, settings, best times and the nickname you type in) is saved only on your own device. It never leaves your device unless you copy your own save code to move it to another phone.

Ads, purchases and tracking

The game has no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking or analytics. Coins and chests are earned by playing and cannot be bought with real money.

Chat and other players

There is no chat. In races, players only see each other's character, nickname and a few fixed emotes.

Children

Because we collect no personal information, the game is suitable for children. Parents can delete all saved progress at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the developer through the email address shown on the game's Google Play page.
